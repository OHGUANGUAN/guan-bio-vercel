About me

Using technology and AI to build tools that improve lives.

"The best technology is the kind that gets out of the way and lets people focus on what truly matters."

I'm Guan, an Engineer in Singapore. My work sits at the intersection of technology and people where I build and develop solutions that help people to work more efficiently.

I believe that great solutions should be useful and provide good user experience. Whether it's automating a repetitive workflow, digitising a paper process, or creating an analytical dashboard that actually makes sense — I focus on creating solutions that people genuinely want to use.

I am proficient in Python, Tableau, Microsoft (M365, Microsoft Power Platform, etc) and Google Workspace Products. I am constantly on the lookout for new technologies and methodologies to enhance my skill set.

Currently, I have an interest to learn to build pro-code solutions with the help of AI and learning on proper cloud solutions deployment. I hope I can continue to build on this interest and hobby. (and this is how this page is born too..)
